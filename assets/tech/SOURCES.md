# Technology logo sources

The logos in the periodic table on `services.html` come from Simple Icons 16.35.0 (https://simpleicons.org, CC0-1.0 for the icon files). They are inlined as an SVG sprite at the top of that page, shown in the brand colour listed below. On hover, the tile takes a light tint of that colour.

The logos themselves are trademarks of their owners. CC0 does not cover trademarks. They are used only to name tools the studio works with. Simple Icons asks users to follow each brand's guidelines and to seek permission where needed (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md). Check the guideline links below before launch.

Tools with no logo in Simple Icons are shown as two-letter symbol tiles: FAISS, PostGIS, OWL/RDF, Pellet, XGBoost, SHAP, LoRA/QLoRA, AutoDock Vina, DiffDock, Boltz-2, RDKit, PennyLane, Playwright and AWS.
